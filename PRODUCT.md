# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Telugu cinema fans and design-conscious streetwear shoppers who recognize popular Telugu movie reactions, characters, and first-day-first-show culture.

## Product Purpose

G Theta sells Telugu meme merchandise through an interactive single-screen theatre experience. Success means visitors enjoy exploring the theatre and can reach the real merchandise without leaving the cinematic world.

## Positioning

The store behaves like a celebratory Hyderabad movie premiere rather than a conventional apparel catalogue.

## Operating Context

Visitors enter through a theatre gate, explore a crowded forecourt, move into a lobby with food and merchandise, and reach an auditorium during a hero-entry celebration. Mouse, touch, wheel, sound, and responsive devices are part of the experience.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Frontend changes must not disturb the concurrently developed backend.
- The forecourt uses the supplied Google Flow frame sequence as a user-controlled 360-degree scene.
- The interface must support mouse, touch, keyboard, reduced motion, and mobile layouts.
- Sound starts only after a visitor gesture because browsers restrict unprompted audio.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: G Theta.
- Red and yellow are the primary brand colors.
- Use real supplied product and people imagery.
- Keep visible interface copy in English rather than Telugu script.
- Preserve the playful, loud, communal energy of Telugu single-screen cinema.

## Evidence on Hand

- User-supplied 360 forecourt frame sequence under `public/images/360/forecourt-sequence/`.
- User-supplied hoodie photography already used by the product catalogue.
- Generated lobby and auditorium environment images under `public/images/360/`.
- User-supplied entry soundtrack under `public/audio/`.

## Product Principles

- Make the theatre the store, not decoration around the store.
- Let the visitor control the camera and pace.
- Keep merchandise discoverable without breaking immersion.
- Prefer culturally specific details over generic ecommerce patterns.
- Preserve fast loading and clear fallbacks despite cinematic presentation.
